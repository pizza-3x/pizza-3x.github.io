We do not collect any personally identifiable information (like name, email, or address) directly from users.

1. Automatically Collected Information

When you access Pizzaedition.pro, certain information may be automatically collected, such as:

This data helps us analyze traffic and improve website performance.

🍪 Cookies

Pizzaedition.pro uses cookies to enhance gameplay and personalize user experience.
 Cookies are small text files stored on your device to remember settings such as sound preferences or game progress.

Types of Cookies Used

  • Essential Cookies: Needed for the site to function correctly.

  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website.

  • Advertising Cookies: Used by third-party partners (like Google AdSense) to show relevant ads.

You can disable cookies through your browser settings, but some site functions may not work properly afterward.
